SAFE CAMPUS COMMUNITY

Acceptable use

The pilot is for invited testing with fictional campus data. Access may be removed when activity puts people or the service at risk.

Use UniCircle to

  • connect respectfully with other test accounts;
  • test messages, calls, circles, events, services and support routes;
  • report faults honestly and protect other people’s privacy.

Do not

  • harass, threaten, impersonate or discriminate against anyone;
  • share passwords, Authenticator codes, financial details or confidential records;
  • publish unlawful, deceptive or copyrighted material without permission;
  • use location, messaging or calls to monitor or pressure another person;
  • attack, scrape, overload or bypass the service’s security controls;
  • submit AI output as assessed work when university rules do not allow it.

Safety

Use the block and report controls when a connection feels unsafe. UniCircle is not an emergency service; contact the appropriate emergency or university safeguarding service when immediate help is needed.
