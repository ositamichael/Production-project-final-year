STAGING PRIVACY INFORMATION

Your privacy in UniCircle

This page describes the current final-year-project pilot. It is not a university privacy notice and must be reviewed by an institution before a public launch.

What the pilot stores

When you create a pilot account, UniCircle stores your email address, password hash, display name and the profile details you choose to provide. The service can also store connection requests, messages, event registrations, bookings, support requests, lost-and-found reports and test results.

Photographs and location

Profile and lost-item photographs are reprocessed before storage to remove embedded metadata. Location sharing is off by default, expires automatically, and is visible only to accepted connections who have also chosen to share. You can stop sharing at any time.

Calls and Ask UniCircle

Voice and video streams travel between participants and are not recorded by UniCircle. Reviewed local Ask UniCircle answers do not leave the application. If you turn on Use public sources, a shortened search topic is sent to English Wikipedia and the returned page is shown as a source. The full conversation is not sent, and obvious personal identifiers and secrets are blocked. A future advanced provider may receive a question only after the same public-source control is enabled. Do not enter passwords, confidential university information or another person’s private data.

Your control

You can edit your profile, stop location sharing, leave circles you do not own, block accounts and sign out. Production retention periods, deletion requests and the formal data controller must be agreed with the deploying university.

Staging rule

Use fictional or low-risk test information only. Never use your real university password or an Authenticator code on the pilot sign-in page.
